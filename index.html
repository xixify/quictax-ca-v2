Is QuicTax.ca NETFILE certified by the CRA?

Yes! QuicTax.ca is fully certified by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) for both NETFILE (personal returns) and EFILE (tax preparers).

How does My AI Tax Assistant work?

Simply upload photos or PDFs of your T4, T5, T3, or W-2 slips. The AI uses OCR technology to parse data fields into CRA forms automatically for your review.

Are there any hidden per-return fees on annual plans?

No! All annual flat plans ($149, $299, $599) include unlimited T1/T2 returns with zero transmission fees.
