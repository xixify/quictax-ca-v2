### General

**How does QuicTax work?**
It's simple: reach out on WhatsApp, share your documents securely, and we handle the rest. Most returns are filed within 48 hours. We'll send you a full summary once your return is submitted.

**Who can use QuicTax?**
Anyone! We serve individuals, freelancers, self-employed professionals, small businesses, and corporations. If you have taxes to file in Canada, we can help.

**How do I get started?**
Just tap the WhatsApp button on our site and send us a message. We'll respond quickly and walk you through exactly what we need from you.

### Filing & Documents

**What documents do I need to provide?**
It depends on your situation. For personal returns: T4 slips, RRSP receipts, and any other income slips. For businesses: income statements, expense records, and prior year returns. We'll give you a personalized checklist after your first message.

**How do I send my documents securely?**
We'll guide you through our secure document sharing process over WhatsApp. Your information is handled with strict confidentiality.

**Can you file returns for previous years?**
Yes. We handle prior year filings and amendments. Whether you're one year behind or several, we can get you caught up and back in good standing with the CRA.

### Pricing & Turnaround

**How much does it cost?**
Pricing depends on the complexity of your return. Contact us on WhatsApp for a quick, no-obligation quote. We're transparent — no hidden fees, ever.

**How fast will my return be filed?**
Most returns are completed within 48 hours of receiving all required documents. Complex corporate returns may take slightly longer, but we'll always give you a clear timeline upfront.

**Do you offer refunds if I'm not satisfied?**
We stand behind our work. If there's an error on our end, we'll fix it at no additional cost. Reach out and we'll make it right.
