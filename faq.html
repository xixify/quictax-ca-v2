Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers about how human-assisted online tax filing works with QuicTax.

Is QuicTax NETFILE certified by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA)?

Yes! QuicTax is fully certified by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) for both NETFILE (personal returns) and EFILE (electronic tax preparers). Your return is transmitted directly to the CRA's secure servers, and you receive an official CRA confirmation number instantly upon filing.

How does human-assisted filing work compared to DIY software?

With DIY tax software, you have to navigate complex tax schedules, enter Box numbers, and guess deduction rules yourself. With QuicTax, you simply send smartphone photos or PDFs of your tax slips to our Canadian tax specialists. We do all calculations, verify deduction limits, and file for you.

What documents do I need to send to get started?

For personal returns, we need your T4 (employment), T4A/T5 (investments/benefits), RRSP contribution receipts, and prior year Notice of Assessment. For self-employed or freelancers, send your revenue totals, expense receipts, and home office details.

How fast will my return be filed?

Our average turnaround time is 24 to 48 hours from the time all required documents are received. Once prepared, we send you a full summary copy for review before submitting directly to the CRA.

Do you handle small business, self-employed, and corporate T2 returns?

Yes! We specialize in T2125 Statements of Business Activities for freelancers/sole proprietors, GST/HST return reconciliations, and complete T2 Corporate Income Tax returns for Canadian Controlled Private Corporations (CCPCs).

What if I have unfiled tax returns from previous years?

We can assist you with prior year back taxes (2018–2025). If you're missing old T4 slips, we can help retrieve your historical tax data directly through the CRA Authorized Representative Portal.

How is my personal data and tax information protected?

We employ bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption and strict data privacy compliance under Canadian PIPEDA standards. Your information is strictly confidential and shared only with certified CRA tax preparers for filing your return.
