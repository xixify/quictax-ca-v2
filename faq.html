How does QuicTax work?

+

It's simple: click our WhatsApp button to connect with a Canadian tax specialist. You share photos or PDF files of your T4s, receipts, or business statements securely. We organize your paperwork, calculate maximum eligible write-offs, and file directly with the CRA via NETFILE. Most returns are completed in 24 to 48 hours.

Who can use QuicTax?

+

We serve individual employees (T1 returns), freelancers, gig workers (Uber, DoorDash, Skip), self-employed contractors, sole proprietorships, small businesses, and incorporated businesses (T2 corporate tax returns) anywhere in Canada.

What documents do I need to provide?

+

For Personal (T1): T4, T4A, T5 slips, RRSP receipts, medical expense receipts, tuition slips (T2202), and your previous Notice of Assessment.
For Self-Employed/Freelancers: Revenue logs, business expense receipts, vehicle mileage logs, and home office costs.
For Corporations (T2): Trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet, and prior T2 return.
