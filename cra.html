Key Filing Deadlines

• April 30: Individual T1 return deadline & payment due date for all taxpayers.
• June 15: Self-employed filing deadline (taxes owed still due April 30).
• 6 Months Post Fiscal Year: Corporate T2 return filing deadline.

📊

2025/2026 Brackets

• 15% on first $57,375
• 20.5% between $57,375 & $114,750
• 26% between $114,750 & $177,882
• 29% between $177,882 & $253,414
• 33% on taxable income over $253,414
