IMPORTANT DATES

Canadian Tax Deadlines You Must Know

Mark these CRA filing and payment deadlines on your calendar to avoid late-filing penalties.

APRIL 30

Personal T1 Tax Deadline

Deadline to file personal T1 income tax returns and pay any outstanding tax balances owed to the CRA.

JUNE 15

Self-Employed Filing Deadline

Filing deadline for self-employed individuals and spouses. (Note: Any tax balance owed is still due April 30).

6 MONTHS AFTER YEAR END

Corporate T2 Tax Deadline

Corporations must file T2 returns within 6 months of corporate fiscal year-end (tax payment due within 2–3 months).
